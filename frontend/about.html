About Us

Our Mission

TutorHub UAE exists to make it simple and safe for students and parents across Dubai and the wider UAE to find qualified private tutors — and for great tutors to build a professional presence and reach families who need them.

Our Story

TutorHub UAE was founded to serve families in Dubai and the wider UAE region, connecting them with vetted, experienced tutors across every subject and level — from early years through university preparation.
